Plant Manager

📍 Sharjah, United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪 💼 Full-time 🏭 Management & Leadership 🎯 Director

Run full P&L, operations and people for a fast-moving consumer-goods manufacturing plant in Sharjah with 200+ staff.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own site safety, quality, output, cost and delivery performance.
  • Lead production, maintenance, QA, warehouse and planning managers.
  • Drive continuous-improvement, OEE and cost-reduction programmes.
  • Ensure food-safety and regulatory compliance (HACCP, ISO 22000).
  • Deliver the annual operating plan and capex projects.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's in Engineering; MBA a plus.
  • 12+ years in manufacturing with 5+ in plant leadership.
  • FMCG / food & beverage manufacturing background preferred.
  • Strong lean / TPM and financial acumen.
  • GCC leadership experience essential.

What's on Offer

  • Executive package with car and housing
  • Family medical and schooling
  • Annual performance bonus
  • Relocation support

HSE Officer

📍 Sitra, Bahrain 🇧🇭 💼 Full-time 🏭 Health, Safety & Environment 🎯 Mid level

Champion a zero-harm culture across a cement and aggregates manufacturing site in Sitra, Bahrain.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct risk assessments, safety inspections and incident investigations.
  • Deliver toolbox talks, inductions and HSE training programmes.
  • Maintain permit-to-work, LOTO and contractor-safety systems.
  • Track and report HSE KPIs and drive corrective actions.
  • Support ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 audits and compliance.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's / Diploma with NEBOSH IGC (IDip a plus).
  • 4+ years HSE experience in manufacturing, cement or heavy industry.
  • Knowledge of GCC labour and environmental regulations.
  • First-aid and fire-warden certification desirable.
  • Strong reporting and communication skills.

What's on Offer

  • Medical insurance
  • Transport allowance
  • Annual leave and ticket
  • Professional development support

Quality Control Engineer

📍 Ras Laffan, Qatar 🇶🇦 💼 Full-time 🏭 Quality & QA/QC 🎯 Mid level

Own product and process quality for a petrochemical plant in Ras Laffan Industrial City, from incoming raw materials to finished polymer batches.

Key Responsibilities

  • Run and interpret lab tests (MFI, density, tensile, ash content) on polymer product.
  • Manage non-conformance reports, CAPA and batch release decisions.
  • Audit process compliance against ISO 9001 and customer specifications.
  • Calibrate and maintain QC lab instruments and documentation.
  • Support customer complaints investigations with data-driven root cause.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's in Chemical Engineering, Polymer Science or Chemistry.
  • 4+ years QA/QC in petrochemicals, plastics or process industry.
  • Working knowledge of ISO 9001 and statistical process control.
  • Experience with lab instrumentation and LIMS.
  • GCC / Qatar experience an advantage.

What's on Offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Family accommodation and schooling support
  • Comprehensive medical cover
  • Annual bonus and tickets

Production Planner

📍 Shuaiba, Kuwait 🇰🇼 💼 Full-time 🏭 Supply Chain & Planning 🎯 Mid level

Build and balance the master production schedule for a food-processing plant in Shuaiba Industrial Area, Kuwait.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create weekly and daily production schedules to meet demand and stock targets.
  • Balance capacity, materials, manpower and changeovers for efficiency.
  • Run MRP in the ERP and release work orders to the shop floor.
  • Coordinate with procurement, warehouse and sales on availability.
  • Track schedule adherence, OEE and inventory KPIs.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's in Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain or related.
  • 4+ years production planning in FMCG / food manufacturing.
  • Strong ERP (SAP / Oracle) and advanced Excel skills.
  • APICS CPIM certification a plus.
  • GCC experience preferred.

What's on Offer

  • Medical insurance
  • Transport allowance
  • Annual leave and ticket
  • Performance bonus

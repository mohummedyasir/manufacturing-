Sohar Aluminium Processing Co.

Mechanical Maintenance Technician

📍 Sohar, Oman 🇴🇲 💼 Full-time 🏭 Maintenance & Reliability 🎯 Associate

Keep casting and extrusion equipment running at a Sohar aluminium plant through preventive and breakdown maintenance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Carry out preventive and corrective maintenance on pumps, gearboxes, conveyors and hydraulics.
  • Diagnose mechanical faults and execute repairs to minimise downtime.
  • Support planned shutdowns and overhauls of major equipment.
  • Maintain accurate work-order records in the CMMS.
  • Follow all HSE, LOTO and confined-space procedures.

Requirements

  • Diploma / ITI in Mechanical or Mechatronics.
  • 3+ years maintenance experience in a heavy manufacturing plant.
  • Hands-on with alignment, bearings, seals and hydraulic systems.
  • Willing to work rotating shifts.
  • Omani nationals and GCC-experienced candidates encouraged to apply.

What's on Offer

  • Housing and transport
  • Medical insurance
  • Shift and overtime allowances
  • Annual leave and ticket

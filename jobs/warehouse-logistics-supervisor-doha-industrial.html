Warehouse & Logistics Supervisor

📍 Doha, Qatar 🇶🇦 💼 Full-time 🏭 Supply Chain & Planning 🎯 Mid level

Run raw-material and finished-goods warehousing and dispatch for an industrial manufacturing operation in Doha.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise receiving, storage, picking and dispatch teams.
  • Maintain inventory accuracy through cycle counts and WMS discipline.
  • Plan inbound and outbound logistics and fleet scheduling.
  • Enforce warehouse safety, 5S and materials-handling standards.
  • Report stock, service-level and cost KPIs.

Requirements

  • Diploma / Bachelor's in Logistics, Supply Chain or Business.
  • 4+ years warehouse / logistics supervision in manufacturing.
  • Hands-on WMS/ERP and forklift-operations knowledge.
  • Strong team leadership and Excel skills.
  • Qatar / GCC experience preferred.

What's on Offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Medical insurance
  • Transport allowance
  • Annual leave and ticket

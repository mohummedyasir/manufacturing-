Assembly Line Operator

📍 Muscat, Oman 🇴🇲 💼 Full-time 🏭 Production & Operations 🎯 Entry level

Assemble electronic products on a modern production line in Rusayl Industrial Estate, Muscat, with full on-the-job training.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assemble components and sub-assemblies to work instructions.
  • Perform in-line visual and functional quality checks.
  • Meet hourly output and first-pass-yield targets.
  • Maintain a clean, ESD-safe and 5S-compliant workstation.
  • Report defects and support continuous improvement.

Requirements

  • High-school diploma or technical certificate.
  • 0–2 years experience; freshers welcome with training.
  • Good hand dexterity and attention to detail.
  • Willing to work in shifts.
  • Omani nationals strongly encouraged to apply.

What's on Offer

  • On-the-job training
  • Transport provided
  • Medical insurance
  • Attendance and productivity incentives

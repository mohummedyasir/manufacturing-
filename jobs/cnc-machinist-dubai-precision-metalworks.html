CNC Machinist (3 & 5-Axis)

📍 Dubai, United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪 💼 Full-time 🏭 Machining & Fabrication 🎯 Associate

Operate and set up CNC milling and turning centres to produce precision components for the oil & gas and aerospace supply chain in Dubai Industrial City.

Key Responsibilities

  • Set up, program (edit) and operate 3- and 5-axis CNC machines (Fanuc / Siemens).
  • Read and interpret engineering drawings and GD&T to tight tolerances.
  • Perform first-article inspection using micrometers, verniers and CMM.
  • Maintain tooling, offsets and machine housekeeping to spec.
  • Meet cycle-time and quality targets with minimal rework.

Requirements

  • ITI / Diploma in Machining, Mechanical or Production Engineering.
  • 3+ years hands-on CNC setting and operating experience.
  • Ability to read G-code and edit programs at the machine.
  • Strong measurement and blueprint-reading skills.
  • Available in the UAE with valid visa or on visit visa.

What's on Offer

  • Company visa and medical insurance
  • Overtime pay
  • Annual leave and air ticket
  • Skill-based increments

Production Supervisor

📍 Jubail, Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦 💼 Full-time 🏭 Production & Operations 🎯 Mid–Senior level

Lead a steel rolling-mill production shift, driving output, quality and safety targets across a 24/7 operation in Jubail Industrial City.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise 25–30 operators across melting, casting and rolling lines during your shift.
  • Hit daily tonnage, yield and OEE targets while minimising downtime and scrap.
  • Enforce HSE standards, LOTO procedures and permit-to-work compliance on the floor.
  • Coordinate with maintenance to schedule preventive work and resolve breakdowns fast.
  • Report shift KPIs and lead daily handover and toolbox-talk meetings.

Requirements

  • Diploma or Bachelor's in Mechanical, Industrial or Metallurgical Engineering.
  • 5+ years in a heavy-industry / steel or metals production environment.
  • Proven people-leadership of a shift team of 20+.
  • Strong knowledge of lean manufacturing, 5S and root-cause analysis.
  • GCC experience and transferable Iqama preferred.

What's on Offer

  • Housing and transport allowance
  • Annual air ticket and 30 days paid leave
  • Medical insurance for self and family
  • Performance bonus

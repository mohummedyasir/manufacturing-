Process Engineer (Chemicals)

📍 Yanbu, Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦 💼 Full-time 🏭 Engineering & Process 🎯 Mid–Senior level

Optimise process performance, yield and energy efficiency for a chemical manufacturing complex in Yanbu Industrial City.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor and troubleshoot continuous chemical process units.
  • Lead debottlenecking, yield and energy-optimisation studies.
  • Prepare and update P&IDs, mass balances and operating procedures.
  • Support HAZOP, MOC and process-safety reviews.
  • Drive plant KPIs and reliability with operations and maintenance.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's in Chemical Engineering.
  • 5+ years process engineering in petrochemicals / chemicals.
  • Proficiency in process simulation (Aspen HYSYS/Plus).
  • Strong process-safety and MOC understanding.
  • GCC experience and transferable Iqama preferred.

What's on Offer

  • Housing and transport allowance
  • Family medical and schooling
  • Annual bonus
  • Leave and tickets

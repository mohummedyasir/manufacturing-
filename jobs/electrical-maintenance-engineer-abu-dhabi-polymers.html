Electrical Maintenance Engineer

📍 Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪 💼 Full-time 🏭 Maintenance & Reliability 🎯 Mid–Senior level

Lead electrical reliability for a polymer manufacturing plant in Abu Dhabi, covering HV/LV, drives, PLCs and instrumentation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own preventive and predictive maintenance for electrical and control systems.
  • Troubleshoot PLCs (Siemens/Allen-Bradley), VFDs, MCCs and switchgear.
  • Lead shutdown electrical scopes and small capex modifications.
  • Manage spares, contractors and compliance with electrical standards.
  • Drive reliability improvements and reduce electrical downtime.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering.
  • 6+ years electrical maintenance in process / petrochemical plants.
  • Strong PLC, instrumentation and HV/LV experience.
  • Knowledge of hazardous-area (ATEX/IECEx) practices.
  • GCC plant experience required.

What's on Offer

  • Housing and transport allowance
  • Family medical insurance
  • Annual bonus
  • Leave and air tickets
